On-Screen User Manual & Help Guide

A plain-language guide for directorate administrators — no technical knowledge required.

✏️ Editing Text (like the Welcome Message or Mission Statement)

1
Find the right tab

Use the sidebar on the left to open the section you want to change — for example, "Home Page" for the Director's message, or "About Framework" for the Mission and Vision text.

2
Click into the box and type

Click directly inside any text box or paragraph field and edit the words exactly as you would in a Word document or a text message.

3
Press the gold "Save" button

Nothing changes on the public website until you press the gold Save button for that section. Once you do, the change appears on the live site within a few seconds.

🔢 Changing Enrollment Numbers

1
Open "Academics & Metrics"

You will see two lists of numbers: Student Enrolment and Staff Counts.

2
Type the new number

Click into the number box next to the category (for example, "Grades 9–12 Regular") and type the new total.

3
Save All Metrics

Press the gold "Save All Metrics" button once you are done updating all the numbers you need to change.

🖼️ Updating a Staff Photo

1
Get the photo file ready

Ask whoever manages the website files to add the new photo into the website folder, and ask them what the file is called (for example, "sichale.jpg").

2
Open "Staff Directory" and find the person

Scroll to find their card in either the Directorates or School Management list, and press "Edit".

3
Type the new filename and Save

Replace the text in the "Photo Filename" box with the new filename exactly as given to you, then press "Save Staff Member".

🗞️ Posting a News Update

1
Open "News Directory"

Fill in the date, a short title, and the full description in plain language — for example, announcing that Grade 8 and Grade 6 regional examinations are complete.

2
Add a photo filename if you have one

This is optional — news items can be text-only.

3
Press "Save News Item"

The new story appears immediately at the top of the News page and in the homepage news feed.
