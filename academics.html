What We Offer

Our Core Programs

Four carefully structured academic tracks serving students from infancy through secondary school.

Early Years

Kindergarten Program

Our KG program offers a bilingual, play-integrated curriculum that lays a strong foundation for lifelong learning. Students are introduced to reading, writing, counting, creative arts, and social interaction in a warm and supportive classroom setting.

Infant & Toddler

Daycare Program

Our daycare program provides professional, attentive care for the youngest members of our community. With trained caregivers and age-appropriate activities, we ensure every child feels safe, stimulated, and cherished.

Primary & Secondary

Grades 1–12 Programs

Our primary and secondary sections combine academic rigour with extracurricular enrichment. Students advance through a structured curriculum with increasing specialisation, culminating in Grade 12 national examinations.
